About

I'm a Full Stack Developer and Co-founder & CTO of Admeasy, an EdTech startup building an admission platform and ERP solution for educational institutions across India.

I build and ship production applications end-to-end — from database design and REST APIs to frontend architecture and deployment. My current focus is on scalable systems, clean backend architecture, and AI integrations that actually add value.

My technical work spans React, Next.js, Node.js, Express, TypeScript, Supabase, PostgreSQL, and REST APIs. I'm particularly interested in the intersection of software engineering and AI — building products where LLMs and agents are a functional part of the system, not a demo.

I prefer building products over shipping projects. The difference is ownership, maintenance, and the pressure of real users.
